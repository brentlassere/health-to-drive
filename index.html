Health to Drive

A personal script that copies one person's fitness data into their own Google Sheet.

What it does

Health to Drive is a single-user Google Apps Script written by Brent Lassere for personal use. Once a day it reads activity, heart-rate and sleep data from the owner's own Fitbit / Pixel Watch account through the Google Health API and writes a daily summary into a Google Sheet in the owner's Google Drive.

Who uses it

Only the author. It is not offered to other users, has no sign-up, and is not a commercial product.

Data handled

  • Daily step counts, calories, heart-rate minimum / maximum / average and resting heart rate
  • Sleep sessions and sleep stages
  • Workout sessions (type, duration, calories, heart-rate zones)

Everything is stored only in the owner's Google account. See the privacy policy.
